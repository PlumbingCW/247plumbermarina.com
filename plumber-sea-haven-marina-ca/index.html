Where Sea Haven sits in Marina

The Marina Heights Specific Plan, dated May 2003, describes about 248 acres of former Abrams Park and Upper Patton Park military housing. The site lies west of Imjin Road and south of Reservation Road. The plan called for demolishing 828 abandoned residential units and building 1,050 new homes in five neighborhoods. The city's specific plans page now lists the project as Sea Haven (Marina Heights).

A 2018 newspaper profile placed Sea Haven in a sandy basin, with the Abrams Park apartments to the west and Imjin Parkway to the south. A builder's sales page describes two neighborhoods named Layia and Villosa, ten neighborhood parks, an 18-acre Sea Haven Park and a clubhouse called The Cove. We use those names only as the city and the builder give them.

Exact street boundaries are not published in the sources we checked, so give us your street address on the call. The 2003 plan also set aside an 8.53-acre oak grove preserve inside the site, which is why some streets here back onto open space.

What newer construction changes for plumbing

Newer pipe is less likely to fail from old age, but age is only one reason people call a plumber. Scale from hard water, a loose supply connection, water pressure set too high and a grease clog all appear in homes of any year. Marina Coast Water District's 2025 report puts the city's water at 10.54 grains per gallon on average, which sits at the edge between hard and very hard on the common USGS scale.

Scale collects on heating elements, tank bottoms and aerators first. That is why a water heater in a young home can still lose output. Our water heater repair page explains what can be fixed and what points to a replacement.

Check these before you book a repair

  • Find your main water shutoff and the sewer cleanout, then photograph both so you can point to them later.
  • Ask your builder about its repair process if the home is recent. California's Right to Repair Act, which starts at Civil Code section 895, sets a notice procedure, so ask the builder or an attorney how it applies to you.
  • Read your association's documents. Shared parks and the clubhouse are normally the association's concern, and lines inside your walls are normally yours.
  • Stop active water first. Shut the valve and limit damage, then deal with paperwork.

Plumbing topics for single-family homes here

These are general plumbing subjects for houses like those in Sea Haven. They are not claims about how often a problem happens in this neighborhood.

  • A high water bill with no change in use usually calls for a leak detection visit, starting with a meter check.
  • A slow drain in one fixture is often a local clog, while several slow drains together point to the main line. See drain cleaning.
  • Adding a laundry sink, an outdoor line or a bathroom is a job for plumbing installation, and it needs a permit.
  • Pressure that is too strong or too weak at every tap can trace to the line from the meter. See water service line repair.
