Central Marina is the established center of the city, around the Reservation Road and Del Monte Boulevard corridor. Real estate sites also call it Downtown Marina. Homes here sit beside shops, apartments and offices, so a plumbing call can mean a house, a rental unit or a small business. Call (855) 575-2890 and tell us which.

This page explains what is documented about the area's housing and how age and mixed use shape plumbing decisions. We offer same-day appointments when available, and you get a clear price before work starts.

What Central Marina is

Marina incorporated on November 13, 1975. Fort Ord, the Army base beside it, closed in 1994, and most of the city's newer neighborhoods, including Sea Haven and The Dunes, were planned after that. Central Marina is the established center next to those projects.

A local real estate guide summarizing the city's plan gives about 2,301 dwelling units in the downtown core, roughly half of them multifamily and the rest a mix of attached and detached single-family homes and duplexes. The Downtown Vitalization Specific Plan was reported adopted in October 2024. It proposes changes to Del Monte Boulevard, including fewer lanes and roundabouts if carried out, so street and utility work may come later. The city can confirm the current status.

How house age enters a plumbing decision

A 2019 draft of the city's housing element reports that about 64 percent of occupied homes citywide were built before 1980, using survey data from 2010 to 2014. Only 11 percent were built before 1960. That is a citywide figure and not a count for Central Marina, and house age alone does not tell you what pipe is in the wall.

What helps is looking. A camera inspection of the sewer lateral shows the pipe material, the joints and any roots or sags. Exposed supply lines show what carries your water. We suggest looking before a remodel, a sale or a repeated clog, then choosing repair or replacement from what the camera shows.

Renters, landlords and small businesses

The 2020 census counted about 56 percent of Marina's occupied homes as rented, so many callers in a mixed area like this are tenants. A tenant can close the fixture valve or the main and report the problem. The owner or manager usually approves the repair, and we explain the work and the price to them if you pass on their contact.

Shops and restaurants along Del Monte Boulevard have needs of their own, such as floor drains and grease control. Our commercial plumbing page covers them.

Call 811 before any digging

California law requires contacting 811 at least two working days before digging, so buried utility lines get marked. This applies to a trench for a sewer or water repair as well as a fence post.
