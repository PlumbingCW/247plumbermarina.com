Older Seaside homes and what age does to pipes

Seaside's own housing plan, a 2020 draft filed with the state, says about 77 percent of the city's housing stock was built before 1980. The draft prints no data year, so treat it as a rough picture rather than an exact count. For a homeowner it means many houses here have plumbing that is decades old, and older pipe is where slow leaks and slow drains tend to start.

The same document notes that Seaside produced a significant amount of low-cost housing for military personnel, and that the base grew from 1968 to 1978. Fort Ord was officially closed in September 1994, according to the US EPA. Homes built in different waves can use different pipe materials, so two neighbors on one block may have very different plumbing. We look at your pipes rather than guess from the street.
