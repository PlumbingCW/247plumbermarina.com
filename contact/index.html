What to have ready when you call

A few details help us send the right plumber with the right parts. You do not need every one of them, and we will ask for anything missing.

  • Your street address and any gate or unit number.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Which fixtures are affected and which still work.
  • Whether you have closed the main water valve.
  • The age and fuel type of the water heater, if that is the issue.
  • The best number to reach you at if the call drops.

Shutoff steps before anyone arrives

  1. Close the main water valve

    Find the valve where the line enters the house, often near the meter or by the water heater. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

  2. Stop using drains

    If sewage or water is backing up, do not run sinks, showers, dishwashers or washing machines.

  3. Shut off the water heater

    If the tank is leaking, turn off its power or gas control and close the cold water supply valve above it.

  4. Stay clear of electricity

    Do not touch outlets, panels or appliances standing in water. Switch the breaker off only if you can reach it dry.

If you smell gas

Do not call us first. Leave the building right away, avoid switches and flames, and call 911 from a safe distance. Then call the gas utility. For PG&E, the number is 1-800-743-5000. Do not return until the utility says it is safe. We can help with plumbing after that.
