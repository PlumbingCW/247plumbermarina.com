Sewage in the house or yard: first steps

  • Stop all water use, including laundry and dishwashers.
  • Do not flush. A broken line cannot carry it away.

Who owns the sewer line? Marina Coast Water District code

According to Marina Coast Water District code, section 5.16.090, side sewers are maintained by the owner of the property they serve. The side sewer is the pipe from your house to the district's main. In plain terms, a broken lateral on your lot is normally your repair, not the district's.

The district owns and operates the pipes and pumps that carry wastewater onward to the Monterey One Water treatment plant. It also asks owners to inspect their laterals and keep them clear of trash and grease. District code on cleanouts calls for them at connections, at bends of 45 degrees or more and at the lateral connection, with spacing no greater than 100 feet. A cleanout near the problem makes diagnosis easier and cheaper.

If you rent, report the problem to your landlord or property manager right away. Marina has more renters than owners, and the owner of record is the one who decides on a repair.

Trenchless lining and replacement, explained

Trenchless work avoids a long trench. For lining, a resin-soaked tube is pulled into the old pipe and cured in place, creating a new pipe inside the old one. For pipe bursting, a tool pulls a new pipe through while breaking the old one outward.

Both methods need the line to be cleaned and surveyed first. A lining will not fix a collapsed section or a deep offset, and a pipe that is crushed flat may need a short dig. We tell you which parts of your run suit trenchless and which do not.

Marina sits on loose, unconsolidated dune sand. In ground like that, a pipe joint can drift open over time, and a continuous liner can be a good answer to open joints. Sandy ground also makes deep trenches wider than they look, so ask how the trench will be shored. Ask about permits too. The City of Marina Building Division at (831) 884-1241 handles building permits for the city.

Cast iron pipe repair

Older homes sometimes have cast iron drain and sewer pipe. It lasts a long time, but it corrodes from the inside, forming rough scale that catches debris and eventually thins the wall. Cracks and holes then let wastewater and roots in.

  • A camera shows scale, pitting and cracks before any cutting begins.
  • A short bad section can often be cut out and replaced with plastic pipe.
  • A long corroded run may suit lining, if the pipe is still round.
  • Heavy corrosion is a poor candidate for hydro jetting, so we do not jet it blindly. See hydro jetting for the safety trade-offs.

If cast iron runs under a slab, lining can avoid breaking concrete, but not every case fits. We say so plainly.

Keeping the sewer line healthy

Flush only human waste and toilet paper, as the district asks. Wipes labeled flushable do not break down. Keep grease out of the sink, since hot water and soap only move it down the line. If you plan to plant trees, keep them well away from the lateral, and have the line scoped if backups start. Our drain cleaning page covers smaller clogs that do not involve the main line.
