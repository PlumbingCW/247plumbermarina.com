Jammed, leaking or backed up, a failed disposal can shut down a kitchen fast.

A disposal that jams, leaks under the sink or backs up into the other basin can stop a kitchen in the middle of dinner. Our emergency garbage disposal repair in Marina is available 24/7, including overnight and holidays. While you wait, switch the disposal off at the wall, stop running water into the sink, and keep your hand out of the drain. If the cabinet is wet, shut the supply stops and place a bucket under the leak.

Most disposal calls are a jam, a leaking seal, or a clog past the unit. We tell you whether a reset, a repair or a replacement makes sense, and the price is clear before any work begins. A same-day appointment may be possible, depending on availability.

Disposal jammed or leaking? Safe steps right now

  • Turn the switch off, then unplug the unit or switch off its breaker before touching anything.
  • Never put your hand in the disposal. Use tongs or pliers to lift out a visible object.
  • If the sink backs up, stop running water and do not use chemical drain cleaners. They can splash and harm seals.
  • For a leak, close the supply stops under the sink and set a bucket below the unit.
  • Keep the dishwasher off. It drains through the disposal and can add water to the problem.

A humming sound with no movement usually means a jam. Silence usually means a tripped reset button or no power. Tell us which one you hear when you call.

Grease, drain lines and what Marina's sewer asks of you

A disposal is only as good as the drain it feeds. Kitchen sinks collect food waste and grease, and grease hardens as it cools in the line. Marina Coast Water District's guidance is plain: never pour fats, oils or grease down a sink or garbage disposal. The district also notes that hot water and soap do not remove grease, because it eventually reforms and solidifies in the pipes.

In practice, run cold water while the disposal works and for a short time after. Skip rice, pasta, coffee grounds, potato peels and fibrous stalks. They pack into a sludge that jams the unit or settles in the trap.

If water backs up after the unit is cleared, the blockage is in the line. Drain cleaning clears most kitchen lines, and repeated backups may call for hydro jetting.

Reset, repair or replace

A reset or freed jam costs the least and is worth trying first. Replacement is the better call when the motor is burned out, the housing leaks, or the unit is older and struggles with ordinary food scraps.

Adding a disposal where there is none

Some kitchens have only a sink strainer. Adding a disposal involves more than bolting on a unit. The sink needs a disposal-ready flange, the dishwasher drain has to connect correctly, and the unit needs a switched outlet or hardwired connection.

If the outlet is missing, an electrician may need to add one. We coordinate the plumbing side and tell you what to arrange. First-time installs are handled as plumbing installation work, with the same clear price before work starts.

What to tell us when you call

A few details let us bring the right parts the first time and give you a clear price before work starts.

  • What you hear when you switch it on: humming, clicking or nothing.
  • Where the water shows up: under the unit, at the top rim or at the pipe.
  • Whether the dishwasher drains through the disposal.
  • Roughly how old the unit is, and whether it is a continuous-feed model.
  • Whether the other side of a double sink also backs up.

If you rent, add your landlord's name and whether they need to approve the work.

Hard water, small kitchens and wear

Local tap water is moderately hard to hard, and scale shows up on disposal flanges and sink fittings over time. Mineral scale builds on sink flanges and gaskets and shortens the life of seals. A disposal that leaks at the top after several years often has a flange or gasket that scale has cracked.

According to MCWD code, plumbing beyond the meter is the customer's responsibility, and leaks must be repaired within 72 hours. A slow weep under the sink is worth fixing before it soaks the cabinet floor.
