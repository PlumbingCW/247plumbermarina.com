Clogged drain right now: what to do first

The first ten minutes matter most. These steps limit the mess and help the technician find the cause quickly.

  1. Stop using every sink, tub, shower, toilet and washing machine in the house.
  2. Check whether only one fixture is slow or several are. Several at once points to a bigger line.
  3. Try a plunger on a sink or tub. Block the overflow opening with a wet rag so the force goes down the drain.
  4. Do not pour in chemical drain cleaner. It sits in standing water and can splash on whoever works on the line next.
  5. Call (855) 575-2890 and describe what you see, hear and smell.

How drain snaking and machine cleaning work

Drain snaking uses a rotating steel cable that breaks through a clog or pulls it back out. For a bathroom sink, a small hand-fed cable is enough. For a kitchen line or a long laundry run, a motorized cable machine gives more reach and torque.

Cables clear a hole through the blockage but can leave the pipe walls coated. If grease or scale lines the pipe, the clog tends to return. In that case we may recommend hydro jetting, which scours the full wall of the pipe instead of punching a channel.

When a camera is the smarter next step

If a cable keeps hitting the same hard stop, or the clog returns within weeks, there may be a belly, a break or a root mass. A sewer camera inspection shows exactly what is there so you pay to fix the real problem.

Why drains clog in Marina homes

Marina Coast Water District reports that its groundwater is moderately hard to hard, averaging about 10.5 grains per gallon in 2025. Hard water tends to make soap scum cling to pipe walls, so tubs and bathroom sinks slow down gradually. Hair then catches on that film and builds the plug.

The district also asks residents to flush only human waste and toilet paper, because wipes sold as flushable do not break down and clog sewer lines. Its guidance on fats, oils and grease is blunt as well. Hot water and soap do not remove grease, because it reforms and solidifies further down the pipe.

Marina sits on loose dune sand. Outdoor drains for patios, garages and driveways collect fine sand and leaves, and roots follow moisture into any weak joint. More than half of Marina households rent, so if a drain keeps clogging in a rental, tell the property manager and note the dates.

Chemical cleaners, baking soda and vinegar

Baking soda and vinegar can nudge a very minor clog on a bathroom sink. Plumbing company guides agree it will not dissolve the grease, hair and soap behind a real blockage, and the two ingredients neutralize each other. Opinions differ on whether vinegar harms older fittings, so do not rely on it for repeated treatment.

Skip caustic drain openers once water is standing. A plunger, a hand snake or removing and cleaning the trap is gentler and gives a technician a safe line to work on. If a drain keeps slowing, a strainer in the shower and a no-grease habit in the kitchen cost almost nothing.

Disposals and toilets have their own causes. For a jammed disposal, see garbage disposal repair. For a toilet that overflows, read about toilet repair.
