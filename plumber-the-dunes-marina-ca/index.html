The Dunes on Monterey Bay is a master-planned community in Marina, just off Highway 1 and Imjin Parkway. It mixes condos, townhomes, duplex-style homes and detached houses. The city lists it in its specific plans as The Dunes (University Village). Call (855) 575-2890 when a leak, clog or water heater problem shows up.

Plumbing in an attached home works differently from plumbing in a house on its own lot. This page explains how, and what to check with your association before you book a repair. We offer same-day appointments when available, and you get a clear price before work starts.

What The Dunes is

Local reporting names Marina Community Partners as the developer of The Dunes and Shea Homes as its builder. A 2018 article said residents were already living there and that about 20 percent of the homes would be priced below market rate. Shea's sales pages describe four collections: Rooftops condos and townhomes of one to four stories, Bay House duplexes, Light House detached homes and Sky House detached homes.

The community sits about nine miles north of Monterey. A shopping center of roughly 365,000 square feet is within walking distance, according to Shea's site, with retailers, restaurants and a movie theater. We cannot publish street boundaries because the sources we checked do not give them, so tell us your address when you call.

Attached homes: who is responsible for what

In a condo, townhome or duplex, a leak does not stay in one unit. Water that starts under a kitchen sink can reach a shared wall or the home below. That makes the shutoff location and your association's rules the first two questions to answer.

Before you book a repair in an attached home

  • Read the association's rules on who may work on shared lines.
  • Warn the neighbor below if water is escaping, and photograph any damage.
  • Ask whether the association wants notice or a licensed contractor for the job.
  • Locate your shutoff valve before you need it.

  1. Close the nearest valve

    Turn off the fixture stop under the sink or toilet. If water keeps coming, close your unit's main shutoff.

  2. Protect the floor and the walls

    Move belongings, soak up standing water and keep power off in any wet area until it is checked.

  3. Tell the people who need to know

    Warn the neighbor below, then contact the association manager and your insurer before cleanup starts.

  4. Call for the repair

    Give us the building address, the unit and where the water is showing. We explain the work and the price before we start.

Detached homes in The Dunes

The Light House and Sky House collections are detached houses, and Shea lists them from about 2,800 to over 4,600 square feet. A larger house generally has longer pipe runs, so a long wait for hot water at a far bathroom is a common question. Fixes range from insulating the line to adding a point-of-use heater or changing the heater itself.

If you are weighing a heater change, see our tankless water heater page for sizing and venting, or water heater installation for planned swaps. For a heater that has already failed, water heater replacement covers the next steps.
