Burst Pipes

Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Marina, CA

Stop the water first, then call for a repair that lasts.

Call (855) 575-2890

A burst pipe can put hundreds of gallons into a home in an hour, so emergency burst pipe repair in Marina, CA starts with one action: shut off the water. Our line is open 24/7 at (855) 575-2890. Close the main valve, keep everyone away from wet outlets and appliances, and open a low faucet to drain what is left in the pipes.

Once the water is off, we take over. A plumber finds the failed section, explains whether it needs a patch or a longer replacement, and gives a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are available when possible for Marina, Seaside, Castroville and nearby Monterey County.

When a burst pipe is an emergency

Treat any pipe that is spraying, pouring or soaking a ceiling as an emergency. A small drip from a joint can wait for a scheduled visit. A split pipe, a failed fitting or a ceiling that sags with water cannot.

  • Water is spraying or running with the fixtures off.
  • A ceiling bulges, drips or has turned dark and soft.
  • Water is reaching light fixtures, outlets, or the panel.
  • The meter spins fast after you close every fixture.
  • A water heater has split and is emptying onto the floor.

Shut it off: step by step

  1. Find the house main

    It is often near the water heater, on an outside wall, or by the meter box. A lever handle closes with a quarter turn. A round handle turns clockwise.

  2. Open a low faucet

    A tub or outside hose bib drains pressure from the pipes and slows the flow.

  3. Stop the water heater

    For a tank that is leaking, close its cold supply valve. Turn electric units off at the breaker and set gas units to off.

  4. Call for help

    If the valve will not turn, call (855) 575-2890. For trouble at the meter itself, the water district's 24-hour line is (831) 883-5900.

The meter and its box belong to the Marina Coast Water District, so do not force them. Your house valve is the one to use.

Electrical and gas safety around the water

Water and electricity are the real danger in the first minutes. Stay out of standing water if any outlet, extension cord or appliance is in it. If the breaker panel is dry and easy to reach, switch off the circuit for the flooded area. If the panel is wet or you are unsure, leave the house and call us from outside.

Gas smell during a flood

If you smell gas or hear hissing, leave the building and call 911 from outside. Then call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000. Do not switch lights on or off, and do not light anything.

What bursts pipes in Marina homes

Winter freezes are the classic cause, but they are not the usual story here. NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Monterey Peninsula airport station, the nearest official station, list an average annual low of 48.9 F. Most local bursts therefore trace to other causes.

  • Worn or corroded pipe that finally splits.
  • A failed fitting, coupling or supply connector.
  • High water pressure that stresses old joints.
  • A nail, screw or shovel strike during other work.
  • A cracked line under a floor, in a wall or in the yard.

Where the pipe sits decides who fixes it. The Marina Coast Water District owns the meter and service connection. The owner maintains the lines on the customer side. A break between the meter and your house is your repair, while a break at the meter box is the district's. The district also requires leaks to be repaired within 72 hours, so move quickly.

Repair options and typical costs

What we do depends on how far the damage runs. A single split in sound pipe can often be cut out and replaced. Pipe that is thin along its whole run may fail again, and a longer replacement can cost less than repeat visits.

Drying out, insurance and next steps

Water damage worsens with time. Move belongings, run fans, and photograph everything before cleanup. According to the California Department of Insurance, a homeowners policy typically covers sudden and accidental water damage, while seepage, leaks, wear and tear and neglect are not. Call your insurer early and read the exclusions. This is not coverage advice.

If water reached walls or flooring, ask a restoration professional to check moisture. If the break came from a slow leak that ran for weeks, our leak detection service helps find other trouble. A break in the yard or under the driveway is covered on our water service line repair page.
