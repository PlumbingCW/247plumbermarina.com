What we are

We are a 24/7 phone line for plumbing problems in Marina and nearby Monterey County. Our job is to pick up, understand what is happening, tell you what to shut off, and get a plumber to your address. We offer a clear price before work starts and same-day appointments when available.

How to check any California plumber's license

Whoever you hire, check the license first. In California, plumbing work is licensed by the Contractors State License Board (CSLB). The plumbing classification is C-36. The board offers an online License Check tool on its website, and you can also call 1-800-321-CSLB (2752).

  • Look up the license on the CSLB site and confirm the status says current and active.
  • Confirm the business name on the record matches the name on the estimate and the truck.
  • Check that the classification fits the work, which is C-36 for plumbing.
  • Look at the bond and workers' compensation details shown on the record.
  • Ask for the license number on written estimates. California rules require it in advertising.

The $1,000 rule

California allows unlicensed people to do small jobs only when the total cost of labor and materials is under $1,000, no building permit is needed and there are no employees. Larger jobs cannot be split into pieces to stay under that line. A water heater replacement needs a permit, so it is never exempt.
